This is not surveillance.

Handover only works with the company's permission and the employee's signed consent, and only on work material the employee chooses to share. They see exactly what is collected, and can withdraw at any time, which deletes everything.

  • A guided interview about their work
  • Work documents they upload or pick from Drive
  • Answers that always show their source
  • No reading of email, chats or files they didn't choose
  • No monitoring of current employees
  • No voice cloning or "digital twin" of the person
