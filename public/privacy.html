Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-06

For the knowledge captured in Handover, the company using Handover is the controller and we are its processor. We process it only on the company's instructions.

What is processed

  • Account details: name, work email, role.
  • For leaving employees: consent records, interview answers, and the work documents they choose to share.
  • Questions asked by the team, the answers, and ratings.
  • Security logs (actions, times, IP addresses).

What is never collected

We never read email, chat or files the employee didn't select. We don't monitor anyone, and we never imitate anyone's voice or personality.

Sub-processors

Google Firebase (login), Google Drive (only if the employee connects it), Anthropic and Voyage AI (AI processing), Postmark (email), and our hosting provider in the region the company chose.

Retention and rights

Data is kept until the company deletes it or the employee withdraws consent. Employees can ask their company, or us, for access to or deletion of their data.
