Terms of service

Version 2026-11-06

1. The service

Handover helps a company capture a leaving employee's work knowledge, with that employee's consent, and lets the company's team ask questions about it.

2. The company's responsibilities

  • Use Handover only with employees who freely consent. Never make consent a condition of pay, references or exit terms.
  • Use it only for work knowledge, never to monitor, evaluate or discipline anyone.
  • Make sure your use follows employment and data protection law where you and your employees are.
  • Keep admin access to the people who need it.

3. Employees

Employees decide what to share, can see what they shared, and can withdraw consent at any time, which deletes their contributions.

4. Answers

Answers are generated from what was shared and may be incomplete or wrong. Check the cited source before acting on anything important.

5. Fees

$299 per departure captured, or a subscription if agreed. Fees are invoiced and payable within 14 days.

6. Ending

You can export and delete your organisation's data at any time in Settings.

7. Liability

To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the fees you paid in the 12 months before the claim.
